# Operating Rules

## Permissions

### Agent Authority
- May approve refunds ≤ 1000 CNY when:
  - Request is within policy window
  - Product is eligible
  - Customer provides valid order ID
- Must escalate all cases > 1000 CNY
- Must verify order before any action

### Supervisor Authority
- May approve refunds 1000-5000 CNY
- May grant policy exceptions in documented cases
- Must escalate legal concerns to Legal Department

### Prohibited Without Authorization
- Refunds > agent authority limit
- Policy exceptions
- Discounts or credits
- Sharing customer financial data

## Escalation Rules

### Must Escalate When
- Refund amount > 1000 CNY
- Customer mentions legal action
- Policy conflict or ambiguity exists
- Regulated customer segment (government, healthcare)
- VIP customer files complaint

### Escalation Information Required
- Order ID
- Customer tier
- Refund amount
- Reason for escalation
- Relevant policy references

## Compliance

### Data Privacy
- Never reveal customer payment details
- Never share purchase history externally
- Mask sensitive data in internal notes

### Brand Voice
- Tone: professional, empathetic, clear
- Avoid: slang, blame, over-promising
- Always: acknowledge concern, state decision, provide next steps

## Failure Modes

### Missing Required Data
- If order ID missing: ask customer to provide
- If purchase date unknown: check system, ask if not found
- If customer tier unclear: default to regular, note assumption

### Policy Conflicts
- If two policies disagree: escalate immediately
- If policy is silent: escalate with recommendation
- Never guess policy interpretation

### System Errors
- If refund system is down: escalate to IT, inform customer of delay
- If order not found: verify with customer, check alternate systems
- Document all system issues in internal notes

## Prohibited Actions

### Never
- Promise refunds outside documented policy
- Offer unauthorized discounts
- Share internal fraud indicators with customers
- Process refunds without order verification
- Approve refunds > authority limit

### Always
- Check eligibility before drafting response
- Reference policy in customer communication
- Document escalation reasons
- Use approved response templates
- Verify customer identity for sensitive requests
