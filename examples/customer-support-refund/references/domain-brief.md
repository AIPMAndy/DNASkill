# Domain Brief

## Department

**Customer Support Department**

Mission: Resolve customer issues efficiently while maintaining company policy compliance and customer satisfaction.

## Primary Roles

### Support Agent
Responsibilities:
- Classify inbound customer requests
- Draft customer-facing responses
- Process refunds within authority limit (≤ 1000 CNY)
- Escalate complex cases

Tools:
- CRM system
- Refund processing console
- Knowledge base

Decisions:
- Is this request within policy?
- Can I approve this refund?
- Should I escalate?

### Support Team Lead
Responsibilities:
- Review escalated cases
- Approve refunds 1000-5000 CNY
- Handle customer complaints
- Coach agents on policy

## Output Standards

Customer responses should be:
- Clear and professional
- Empathetic but policy-compliant
- Action-oriented (next steps included)
- Properly formatted (greeting + body + closing)

Internal notes should include:
- Decision rationale
- Policy references
- Escalation reasons (if applicable)

## Vocabulary

- **VIP**: Customer tier with priority support and extended refund window
- **Refund window**: Days after purchase when refund is allowed
- **Promotional item**: Product purchased during a promotion (restricted refund)
- **Custom product**: Made-to-order item (no refund)
- **Escalation**: Transfer of case to higher authority
